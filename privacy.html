VKS Tech ("we") respects your privacy. This policy explains how MetPower collects, uses, and protects your information. By using MetPower (web app + Play Store app), you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 You Provide

  • Mobile Number — for OTP login (mandatory)
  • Full Name & Designation — for identification
  • Company / Plant Name & Type — to create your workspace
  • Plant Location — city/state for support purposes
  • Equipment Count — number of metallisers and slitters at your plant
  • Employee Code — your internal company identifier
  • Schedule, Leave, NCR, Report data — created by you or your manager

1.2 Automatic

  • Device Info — browser, OS version, screen size
  • Usage Logs — login time, last active time
  • Crash Reports — anonymous error data

1.3 We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ Contacts, photos, location (GPS), microphone, camera (unless you explicitly use a feature)
  • ❌ Banking or financial information
  • ❌ Cross-app tracking
  • ❌ Browsing history outside MetPower

2. How We Use Information

  • OTP-based authentication via Firebase Phone Auth
  • Provide shift schedule, leave, NCR, reporting features
  • Send WhatsApp notifications to your registered number when shifts change
  • Enable manager–team coordination
  • Provide Learn & Grow modules (Met Train, MetCost, MRM, Time Study, SupSkill)
  • Detect and prevent abuse, fraud, unauthorized access
  • Comply with legal obligations under Indian law

3. Data Storage & Security

Data is stored on Google Firebase Realtime Database in asia-southeast1 (Singapore) region with industry-standard encryption.

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit
  • Firebase Phone Auth + reCAPTCHA protection
  • Multi-tenant security rules: each company can access only its own data
  • Cross-company access prevented at database level
Note: No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We follow best practices but cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Limited sharing only:

  • Within your company: Manager sees team schedules; team members see their own + roster
  • Service Providers: Google Firebase (hosting, auth, database), Fast2SMS / Firebase SMS (OTP delivery), WhatsApp (you click links — not auto-sent by us)
  • Legal Compliance: if required by Indian court order or law enforcement
  • Business Transfer: if VKS Tech is acquired/merged, data transfers with same protections

5. Learn & Grow Module Data

The Learn & Grow section embeds VKS Tech's training tools (Met Train PRO, MetCost Pro, MRM, Time Study, SupSkill). These run as iframes within MetPower.

  • Each module may store its own user preferences locally on your device
  • Quiz scores in Met Train PRO are stored on your device only (not uploaded)
  • MetCost calculations are session-only — not saved to the cloud
  • No cross-module tracking
