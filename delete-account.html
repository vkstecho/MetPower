🗑️ Account Deletion

How to delete your MetPower account and data

This page explains how to permanently delete your MetPower account, what data is removed, what may be retained, and how long the process takes. Provided in compliance with Google Play Store data safety requirements and India's DPDP Act 2023.

How to Delete Your Account

1In-App (Recommended)
  1. Open MetPower and log in with your registered mobile number
  2. Tap the 👤 Profile button (top-right)
  3. Scroll down → tap 🗑️ Delete My Account
  4. Read the warning carefully
  5. Type the word DELETE (in capitals) to confirm
  6. Tap Delete My Account Permanently

⏱ Processing: Immediate

What Gets Deleted

If You Are a Team Member

  • ✅ Your profile (name, mobile, employee code) permanently removed
  • ✅ Login credentials revoked immediately
  • ✅ Personal usage logs removed
  • ⚠️ Historical schedule entries created by your manager remain in the company's records (anonymized — your name shown as "Deleted User")

If You Are a Manager

⚠️ IMPORTANT: Deleting a Manager account permanently deletes the entire company workspace, including all team members, schedules, leaves, NCRs, reports, and to-do items. This cannot be undone.
  • ✅ Your profile permanently removed
  • ✅ All employees you registered are removed
  • ✅ All schedule data, leave applications, NCRs, reports deleted
  • ✅ All team members lose access immediately
  • ✅ Company workspace completely purged from MetPower servers

To transfer ownership instead of deleting, contact us before requesting deletion.

FAQ

Can I delete only some of my data?

Yes — for partial deletion (e.g. delete a specific shift entry but keep your profile), email us specifying what you want removed.

Will my mobile be available for re-registration?

Yes. After complete deletion, you can register again with the same mobile number as a new user.

What about WhatsApp messages I've received?

WhatsApp messages are stored on your device and WhatsApp's servers, not ours. Deleting MetPower doesn't delete WhatsApp history. Delete those directly via WhatsApp.

Can someone else request deletion of my account?

No. Only you (verified via OTP to your registered mobile) or our Grievance Officer (in case of court order) can request deletion.
